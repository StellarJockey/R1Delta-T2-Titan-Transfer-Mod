**READ THIS BEFORE INSTALLING**

This mod fuses both Yoshi's Titan Transfer mod and Aphelion's Frontier Overhaul mod. These new Titans are available in your Titan loadouts menu and will also spawn as friendly/hostile NPC Titans, just like in regular FO. The only Titans missing from TF|2 are Tone and Monarch. 

HOW TO GET THE NEW TITAN TEXTURES TO WORK:
-
* Go into \R1Delta-T2-Titan-Transfer-Mod\materials\models\titans
* Inside each of the 3 Titan folders are the textures, all compressed into zips
* Simply extract ALL of the zips' contents out into the same place as the txt
